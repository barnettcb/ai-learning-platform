# Core Curriculum Map

## Part I — Understand the Tool
1. What AI can and cannot do
2. How conversational AI produces answers
3. Why confident answers can still be wrong
4. When AI is useful
5. When AI should not be used
6. Privacy, sensitivity, and safe sharing

## Part II — Communicate Clearly
7. Define the result you need
8. Provide relevant context
9. State constraints and boundaries
10. Specify audience, tone, and format
11. Break complex work into manageable steps
12. Ask for assumptions and uncertainties
13. Use examples effectively
14. Continue the conversation instead of restarting
15. Diagnose and improve weak results

## Part III — Apply the Skill
16. Research and information gathering
17. Verification and source evaluation
18. Writing and rewriting
19. Summarizing and extracting information
20. Planning and project management
21. Comparing options and making decisions
22. Financial learning and analysis
23. Medical information and appointment preparation
24. Education and skill building
25. Hobbies, travel, and everyday life
26. Working with uploaded files
27. Working with images
28. Using web-connected AI

## Part IV — Build Reliable Workflows
29. Choose the right AI approach
30. Reuse prompts without becoming rigid
31. Build checklists and templates
32. Manage long-running projects
33. Preserve context and create handoffs
34. Review outputs systematically
35. Combine human judgment with AI assistance
36. Create a personal AI workflow

## Capstone
Plan, execute, verify, and document a real multi-step project using the full decision and collaboration framework.
