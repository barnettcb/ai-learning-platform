# Module Blueprint

The core curriculum is organized into twelve modules. Each module contains three focused lessons and ends with a practical completion task. The thirty-six canonical lessons in `core-map.md` remain the authoritative concept homes.

## Part I — Understand the Tool

### Module 1 — What AI Is Good At
- Lesson 1: What AI can and cannot do
- Lesson 2: How conversational AI produces answers
- Lesson 3: Why confident answers can still be wrong
- Completion task: Sort realistic tasks by suitability and explain the risks.

### Module 2 — Use AI Deliberately
- Lesson 4: When AI is useful
- Lesson 5: When AI should not be used
- Lesson 6: Privacy, sensitivity, and safe sharing
- Completion task: Apply a use-or-don't-use decision guide to five scenarios.

## Part II — Communicate Clearly

### Module 3 — Define the Assignment
- Lesson 7: Define the result you need
- Lesson 8: Provide relevant context
- Lesson 9: State constraints and boundaries
- Completion task: Turn an unclear request into a usable task brief.

### Module 4 — Shape the Response
- Lesson 10: Specify audience, tone, and format
- Lesson 11: Break complex work into manageable steps
- Lesson 12: Ask for assumptions and uncertainties
- Completion task: Design a multi-step request with visible assumptions.

### Module 5 — Improve Through Conversation
- Lesson 13: Use examples effectively
- Lesson 14: Continue the conversation instead of restarting
- Lesson 15: Diagnose and improve weak results
- Completion task: Repair a weak AI exchange without rewriting from scratch.

## Part III — Apply the Skill

### Module 6 — Research Reliably
- Lesson 16: Research and information gathering
- Lesson 17: Verification and source evaluation
- Lesson 28: Using web-connected AI
- Completion task: Produce and verify a short research brief.

### Module 7 — Work With Information
- Lesson 19: Summarizing and extracting information
- Lesson 26: Working with uploaded files
- Lesson 27: Working with images
- Completion task: Extract, compare, and summarize information from mixed inputs.

### Module 8 — Write, Learn, and Apply
- Lesson 18: Writing and rewriting
- Lesson 24: Education and skill building
- Lesson 25: Hobbies, travel, and everyday life
- Completion task: Complete a writing workflow, an active-learning workflow, and a low-stakes everyday application using distinct instructions and review methods.

### Module 9 — Plan and Decide
- Lesson 20: Planning and project management
- Lesson 21: Comparing options and making decisions
- Lesson 29: Choose the right AI approach
- Completion task: Build a plan, compare alternatives, and justify the chosen AI approach.

### Module 10 — Use AI in High-Stakes Domains
- Lesson 22: Financial learning and analysis
- Lesson 23: Medical information and appointment preparation
- Lesson 35: Combine human judgment with AI assistance
- Completion task: Distinguish education, preparation, decision support, and professional advice.

## Part IV — Build Reliable Workflows

### Module 11 — Make Good Work Repeatable
- Lesson 30: Reuse prompts without becoming rigid
- Lesson 31: Build checklists and templates
- Lesson 34: Review outputs systematically
- Completion task: Convert one successful interaction into a reusable, reviewed workflow.

### Module 12 — Manage Ongoing Work
- Lesson 32: Manage long-running projects
- Lesson 33: Preserve context and create handoffs
- Lesson 36: Create a personal AI workflow
- Completion task: Design a durable workflow for a real recurring project.

## Capstone — Complete a Real Project
Learners choose a meaningful, non-trivial task and complete the full cycle:
1. Decide whether and how AI should be used.
2. Define the desired outcome.
3. Provide context and constraints.
4. Select the appropriate tools and inputs.
5. Work iteratively.
6. Review and verify the result.
7. Document what should be reused and what still requires human judgment.
