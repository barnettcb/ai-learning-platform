# Curriculum Dependency Map

## Part I
- Lesson 1 is the entry point.
- Lesson 2 explains behavior observed in Lesson 1.
- Lesson 3 depends on the mental model from Lesson 2.
- Lessons 4 and 5 depend on Lessons 1 and 3.
- Lesson 6 depends on task suitability from Lessons 4 and 5.

## Part II
- Lesson 7 begins the communication sequence.
- Lessons 8 and 9 depend on a defined result from Lesson 7.
- Lesson 10 depends on Lessons 7–9.
- Lesson 11 depends on a defined result and constraints.
- Lesson 12 depends on the limitation model from Lessons 2 and 3.
- Lesson 13 depends on context and output definition.
- Lesson 14 depends on Lessons 7–13.
- Lesson 15 integrates all prior communication lessons.

## Part III
- Lesson 16 depends on task definition and uncertainty awareness.
- Lesson 17 depends on Lesson 3 and follows Lesson 16.
- Lesson 28 depends on Lessons 16 and 17.
- Lesson 19 depends on Lessons 7–10.
- Lessons 26 and 27 depend on Lesson 19 plus privacy from Lesson 6.
- Lesson 18 depends on Lessons 7–10 and 14–15.
- Lessons 24 and 25 depend on the communication sequence but introduce no new prompt fundamentals.
- Lesson 20 depends on decomposition from Lesson 11.
- Lesson 21 depends on assumptions from Lesson 12.
- Lesson 29 integrates task suitability and application workflows.
- Lessons 22 and 23 depend on verification, privacy, and human accountability.
- Lesson 35 follows the two high-stakes domain lessons.

## Part IV
- Lesson 30 depends on successful iterative work from Lesson 15.
- Lesson 31 depends on Lesson 30.
- Lesson 34 depends on verification and application experience.
- Lesson 32 depends on decomposition, iteration, and approach selection.
- Lesson 33 depends on Lesson 32.
- Lesson 36 integrates the entire curriculum.
