# Module 2 Completion Task — Decide Before You Share

## Instructions
For each scenario, choose one action:
- Use AI normally
- Use AI with safeguards
- Use another source first
- Do not use AI for this task

Then identify:
1. Why that action fits
2. What information, if any, may be shared
3. The safest next step

## Scenarios
1. Create five names for a fictional character.
2. Review a confidential employee investigation file in a personal AI account.
3. Prepare questions for a doctor using a short, de-identified symptom summary.
4. Determine whether a person with severe chest pain should drive themselves to a clinic.
5. Compare vacation ideas using general preferences and a budget range.

## Evaluation criteria
A successful response:
- distinguishes convenience from suitability;
- directs emergencies and urgent risks to appropriate real-world help;
- uses official or professional sources where they control the outcome;
- minimizes sensitive information before sharing;
- keeps consequential judgment with the responsible person or professional.

## Suggested evaluation
1. **Use AI normally.** This is low risk and requires no private information.
2. **Do not use AI for this task** in a personal account. Use the organization's approved process and authorized systems.
3. **Use AI with safeguards.** Remove identifying details and use the result only to prepare for professional care.
4. **Use another source first.** Follow emergency guidance and contact emergency services; do not delay for AI advice.
5. **Use AI normally**, while independently confirming current prices, availability, and restrictions before booking.
