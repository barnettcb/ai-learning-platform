# Module 6 Completion Task — Produce a Verified Research Brief

## Scenario
A community group is considering replacing printed meeting notices with email and text-message reminders. It wants to understand accessibility, cost, participation, and privacy considerations before deciding.

## Instructions
Create a short research workflow and final brief that:
1. states a focused research question and scope;
2. identifies the evidence categories needed;
3. uses current, appropriate sources where facts may change;
4. distinguishes source-supported findings from assumptions;
5. identifies conflicting evidence or missing information;
6. verifies the claims that materially affect the decision;
7. ends with findings and open questions, not an overstated conclusion.

## Evaluation criteria
A successful submission:
- treats AI as an assistant rather than the evidence;
- uses sources suited to the claims;
- checks whether citations actually support the statements;
- considers date, jurisdiction, and source independence;
- makes uncertainty visible;
- remains concise enough for the group's decision-makers.

## Required review note
Include a brief note naming the two claims that received the strongest verification and explaining why they deserved priority.
