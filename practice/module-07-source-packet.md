# Module 7 Fictional Source Packet

Use this packet only for the Module 7 applied task. The material is fictional and intentionally contains qualifications, one inconsistency, and one detail that cannot be confirmed from the image.

## Source A — Written proposal

### Page 1 · Section 1: Proposed scope
The Green Court Neighborhood Association proposes replacing the unused east-lawn seating area with a shaded gathering space. The proposal includes a 16-by-20-foot concrete pad, one fixed picnic table, two movable benches, and a fabric shade canopy. Existing trees are to remain.

### Page 1 · Section 2: Schedule and responsibility
The contractor estimates two working days for demolition and base preparation, followed by one working day for concrete placement. The concrete must cure for at least seven calendar days before furniture installation. The association will obtain owner approval and coordinate resident notices. The contractor will obtain any trade permits specifically required for its work.

### Page 2 · Section 3: Qualifications and exclusions
The proposal assumes normal soil conditions and clear access from the east service gate. Irrigation relocation, electrical work, drainage correction, tree-root remediation, and after-hours work are excluded. The shade-canopy fabric color will be selected after approval. Pricing remains valid for 30 days.

### Page 2 · Section 4: Stated total
The written proposal states a total price of **$18,450**.

## Questions the sources leave open

- Which total is intended: the proposal’s $18,450 or the table’s $18,950?
- Are permits required, and if so, who pays the fees?
- Is the east service gate wide enough for the planned access?
- Will the drain inlet or irrigation line conflict with the proposed pad?
- Has an arborist or other qualified person reviewed the tree-root area?
