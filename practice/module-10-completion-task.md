# Module 10 Completion Task — Build a High-Stakes Information Workflow

## Choose one domain
Use either a financial or medical scenario. It may be real with sensitive details removed, or fictional but realistic.

## Required workflow
Create a complete process that includes:
1. the question and its practical purpose;
2. the limited role assigned to AI;
3. the minimum information needed;
4. privacy protections;
5. supplied evidence and facts requiring current verification;
6. assumptions and uncertainties;
7. questions for the appropriate professional;
8. the responsible human decision-maker;
9. the checks required before action.

## Domain-specific requirement
For a financial scenario, independently verify any calculation, product term, tax rule, or legal limit that affects the conclusion.

For a medical scenario, separate observations, report language, general education, urgency concerns, and clinician decisions.

## Evaluation criteria
A successful submission:
- keeps AI in a supporting role;
- avoids unsupported diagnosis or personalized financial direction;
- minimizes sensitive information;
- distinguishes supplied evidence from generated explanation;
- uses current authoritative sources where facts may change;
- names the reviewer and decision owner;
- prevents action until the required human checks are complete.

## Required review note
Describe the most serious plausible harm if the AI output were wrong and the safeguard that reduces that risk.
