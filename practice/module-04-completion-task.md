# Module 4 Completion Task — Design a Controlled Multi-Stage Request

## Scenario
A neighborhood association wants to decide whether to start a monthly email newsletter. The organizers have informal ideas but have not agreed on audience, content, workload, or ownership.

## Instructions
Create a staged AI request that:
1. identifies the people the final recommendation is for;
2. requests an appropriate tone and format;
3. separates discovery, planning, and final recommendation stages;
4. requires assumptions and unanswered questions to be visible;
5. includes at least one human review checkpoint.

Do not use examples as a prompting technique and do not ask AI to verify external sources. Those skills are taught later.

## Evaluation criteria
A successful design:
- shapes the response for a real audience;
- uses stages because later work depends on earlier decisions;
- places a review checkpoint before the final recommendation;
- distinguishes facts, assumptions, and missing information;
- does not divide simple actions into unnecessary micro-steps.

## Sample structure
1. Organize the current ideas and identify missing decisions. Label known facts, assumptions, and questions.
2. Pause for organizer corrections.
3. Propose two practical newsletter operating models, including workload and ownership.
4. After a model is selected, prepare a concise recommendation for the association board in a neutral, direct format.
