Verification Ladder

Match the verification effort to the consequence of being wrong.

Level 1 — Sense-check

Use for brainstorming, private drafts, and low-impact ideas.

  • Look for obvious contradictions.
  • Confirm that the response addresses the request.

Level 2 — Source check

Use for factual work that will be shared or acted on.

  • Confirm important claims against reliable sources.
  • Check dates, names, calculations, and quoted material.
  • Distinguish sourced facts from AI-generated interpretation.

Level 3 — Authoritative confirmation

Use for high-stakes decisions.

  • Consult authoritative records, current rules, or qualified professionals.
  • Preserve the supporting evidence.
  • Require responsible human approval before action.

Best practice

Do not verify every sentence equally. Identify the claims that could materially change the result and check those first.
