AI Use Decision Guide

Use this guide before beginning a task.

1. Is the task suitable for AI assistance?

Use AI when it can help explain, organize, draft, compare, transform, or generate possibilities.

Do not delegate responsibility when the task requires direct observation, confidential judgment, professional authority, or a guaranteed factual result.

2. What role should AI play?

Choose one primary role:

  • explain;
  • brainstorm;
  • draft;
  • revise;
  • summarize;
  • extract;
  • compare;
  • plan;
  • review.

3. What information is required?

Provide only the facts, files, examples, preferences, and constraints needed for the task. Remove sensitive information that is unnecessary.

4. How current must the answer be?

Use current sources when facts may have changed. Use supplied source material when accuracy depends on a specific document.

5. What level of review is required?

  • Low: private brainstorming or rough drafts.
  • Moderate: work shared with others or used to guide a routine decision.
  • High: health, money, law, employment, safety, or irreversible action.

6. Who makes the decision?

AI may support the work. A responsible person remains accountable for verification, approval, and action.
