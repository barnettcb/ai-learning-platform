Core AI Workflow — Decide, Define, Provide, Collaborate, Review, Preserve

Decide

  • Is AI suitable for this task?
  • What role should it play?
  • What must remain with a person or professional?
  • What information should not be shared?

Define

  • What result is needed?
  • Who will use it?
  • What boundaries and success criteria apply?
  • Which assumptions must be visible?

Provide

  • What facts, files, images, examples, or current sources are necessary?
  • Which information is authoritative?
  • What is still missing?

Collaborate

  • Should the task be done directly, in stages, or through revision?
  • What should be clarified before proceeding?
  • Which decisions need to be recorded?

Review

  • Did the result solve the right problem?
  • Are facts, calculations, and sources reliable?
  • What is incomplete or uncertain?
  • What risk exists if the output is wrong?
  • Who approves real-world use?

Preserve

  • Save the final result outside the conversation.
  • Retain reusable templates and checklists.
  • Record approved decisions and unresolved issues.
  • Create a handoff when work will continue later.
