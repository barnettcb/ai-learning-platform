What Boundaries Should I State?

What you will be able to do

You can state the limits and requirements that a useful result must respect.

The key idea

Constraints are conditions the result must satisfy. They narrow the solution space and prevent suggestions that are unusable even if they sound reasonable.

Common constraints include:

  • time available;
  • budget;
  • deadline;
  • length;
  • required or prohibited items;
  • available tools;
  • location;
  • legal, policy, or safety limits;
  • level of complexity;
  • decisions that are already fixed.

Separate facts from boundaries

Context describes the situation. Constraints define what the result must respect.

Example:

  • Context: “The team has six members.”
  • Constraint: “The meeting must fit within 30 minutes.”

Both matter, but they perform different jobs.

Example

Result

“Create a plan for organizing the garage.”

Useful constraints

  • finish over two weekends;
  • spend no more than $250;
  • do not install permanent wall fixtures;
  • keep one parking space usable at all times;
  • use storage bins already owned where practical.

Without these boundaries, the AI may suggest a costly renovation or a plan that disrupts the space for weeks.

Best practice State the few constraints that would make an otherwise good answer unusable.

Watch for this A learner lists preferences as if they were absolute requirements. Too many rigid constraints can eliminate useful options or produce a forced answer.

Use clear labels when helpful:

  • Required
  • Preferred
  • Avoid

Try it

Sort each item for a work presentation into required, preferred, or avoid.

  • Must fit in 10 minutes
  • Prefer no more than six slides
  • Must include the approved revenue figure
  • Avoid technical jargon
  • Prefer a short question period

There is no universal answer beyond the items already labeled “must.” The exercise is to make the strength of each boundary explicit rather than treating every preference as equal.

Apply it

Add boundaries to the task from Lessons 7 and 8. Include only conditions that materially affect whether the result is usable. Label preferences when they are not mandatory.

Check yourself

You are ready to continue when your task makes clear what the result must respect without overconstraining how the AI gets there.

Pause and decide

Which is a useful boundary?
