How Do I Repair a Weak Result?

What you will be able to do

You can identify why an answer failed and issue a targeted correction instead of merely asking AI to “try again.”

The key idea

Diagnose the failure before requesting a revision.

Most weak results fail in one or more of these ways:

  • wrong result;
  • missing or misunderstood context;
  • ignored constraint;
  • unsuitable depth or format;
  • unsupported claim;
  • weak organization;
  • excessive length;
  • important uncertainty hidden;
  • factual error.

A practical repair pattern

Use four moves:

  1. Identify what works.
  2. Name the specific problem.
  3. Correct the instruction or missing information.
  4. Request a focused revision.

Example:

“The comparison categories are useful. The recommendation, however, assumes cost is the highest priority. Reliability is the highest priority, followed by cost. Re-evaluate only the recommendation and explain how the ranking changes.”

Do not rely on vague dissatisfaction

Instructions such as these provide little direction:

  • “Make it better.”
  • “Try again.”
  • “This is not what I wanted.”
  • “Be more accurate.”

A useful correction makes the mismatch visible.

Example

Weak output

A project update contains three pages of background and no clear request for leadership.

Targeted repair

“Keep the milestone and risk information. Remove the project history. Begin with a two-sentence status summary, then list the decision leadership must make, the deadline for that decision, and the consequence of delay.”

The repair changes the function of the document, not merely its wording.

Best practice Revise the smallest layer that is actually broken. Do not replace a good structure because one paragraph is weak.

Watch for this Learners confuse factual correction with stylistic revision. When a claim may be wrong, do not simply ask for more confident wording. Mark it for verification or supply the correct fact.

Try it

Diagnose this result:

The learner asked for a beginner explanation. The answer is accurate but uses unexplained specialized terms.

Diagnosis: unsuitable assumed knowledge and depth.

Targeted correction:

“Keep the core explanation, but rewrite it for a complete beginner. Define each necessary technical term in plain language when it first appears, and remove terms that are not needed.”

Apply it

Choose a weak AI response. Label the failure category, identify what should be preserved, and write a focused repair request.

Check yourself

You are ready to continue when your revision request explains the mismatch clearly enough that a different person could carry out the correction.
