Module 9 Completion Task — Plan, Compare, and Choose an Approach

Scenario

Select a real decision that requires several steps and at least three plausible alternatives. Examples include planning an event, choosing a service, organizing a household project, or selecting a learning program.

Part A — Approach statement

Before using AI, record:

  1. the result you need;
  2. the information required;
  3. whether that information must be current or document-specific;
  4. the AI approach you will use;
  5. the decisions that remain human responsibilities.

Part B — Project plan

Create a plan that includes:

  • major work areas;
  • dependencies;
  • owners or responsible roles;
  • decision points;
  • assumptions and missing information;
  • risks and responses;
  • target dates only where they can be supported.

Part C — Option comparison

Compare at least three alternatives using:

  • defined criteria;
  • visible priorities;
  • sourced facts or supplied information;
  • separate assumptions;
  • important tradeoffs;
  • a conditional or justified recommendation.

Evaluation criteria

A successful submission:

  • selects an AI approach that fits the required evidence;
  • distinguishes a draft plan from confirmed commitments;
  • does not hide invented dates or resources;
  • separates facts, assumptions, and preferences;
  • makes tradeoffs understandable;
  • preserves human ownership of the final decision.

Required review note

Identify one way the recommendation changed, or should have changed, when an assumption or priority changed.
