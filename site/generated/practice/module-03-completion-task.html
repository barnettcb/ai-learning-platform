Module 3 Completion Task — Build a Usable Task Brief

Starting request

“Help me plan a community event.”

Instructions

Rewrite the request as a short task brief containing:

  1. The result needed
  2. Relevant context
  3. Required constraints
  4. Any preferences clearly labeled as preferences

Do not specify audience, tone, response format, examples, or step-by-step procedure. Those skills are taught later.

Sample scenario details

  • The event is for approximately 40 neighborhood residents.
  • The purpose is to help new and longtime residents meet.
  • The available budget is $600.
  • The event must occur on a Saturday afternoon next month.
  • The community room is available for three hours.
  • Food must include at least one vegetarian option.
  • Organizers prefer activities that do not require special equipment.

Evaluation criteria

A successful task brief:

  • states a visible result rather than only naming the topic;
  • includes facts that change the answer;
  • distinguishes requirements from preferences;
  • omits irrelevant story and premature instructions;
  • is short enough that the main assignment remains easy to find.

Example of a successful brief

“Create a practical event plan for a three-hour neighborhood gathering that helps approximately 40 new and longtime residents meet. The event will be held in the community room on a Saturday afternoon next month. The total budget is $600, and food must include at least one vegetarian option. Prefer activities that require no special equipment.”
