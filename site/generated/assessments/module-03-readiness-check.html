Module 3 Readiness Check

Identify what makes an assignment clear enough for useful AI work.

Choose one answer for each scenario, then submit the check. Explanations appear after each attempt, your latest result is saved, and a perfect score marks the check complete.

1. Which instruction is most likely to produce a useful first draft?

A useful assignment states the task, audience, purpose, constraints, and desired output.

Clarification is safer than silent assumptions when missing information materially affects the work.
