# Lesson 18 — How Should I Use AI for Writing and Rewriting?

## Outcome
You can use AI at the appropriate stage of a writing task while preserving purpose, facts, voice, and responsibility for the final text.

## Direct answer
Give AI a defined writing role instead of asking it to replace the entire writing process.

AI can help with:
- brainstorming;
- outlining;
- organizing rough notes;
- drafting;
- rewriting for a specific reader;
- shortening or expanding;
- improving clarity;
- checking consistency;
- identifying unanswered questions;
- comparing alternate versions.

The best starting role depends on what you already have.

## Start from the real state of the work
Choose the instruction that matches your situation:
- no material yet → brainstorm or outline;
- rough ideas → organize without inventing facts;
- complete draft → revise against stated goals;
- sensitive message → identify risks before rewriting;
- final version → proofread and check for unsupported claims.

## Define what must remain true
Writing instructions should identify:
- the purpose;
- the reader;
- the desired action or outcome;
- facts that must be included;
- facts that must not be invented;
- tone and formality;
- length or format;
- language that must be preserved or avoided.

## Worked example
### Weak request
“Make this sound better.”

### Better request
“Rewrite this update for a client who is concerned about schedule. Keep every date and factual commitment unchanged. Use calm, direct language, explain the cause without sounding defensive, and end with the next action and responsible party.”

The revised request defines what “better” means.

## Keep authorship visible
AI-generated prose may sound polished while becoming generic, overstated, or unlike the person sending it. Review whether the final version:
- says what you actually believe;
- makes promises you can keep;
- preserves necessary nuance;
- sounds appropriate for the relationship;
- contains only verified facts;
- reflects your own judgment.

## Best practice
Use separate passes for substance and style. First make the content correct and complete; then improve wording, tone, and flow.

## Common failure
Learners repeatedly ask for a more professional or friendly tone without defining the reader, purpose, or problem. Each rewrite changes the surface while leaving the communication goal unresolved.

## Guided practice
Improve this request:

“Write an email asking for more time.”

One possible revision:

“Draft a concise email to a project manager requesting a three-business-day extension. State that two supplier confirmations are still pending, explain that the extension will improve pricing accuracy, propose the new submission date, and avoid blaming the suppliers.”

## Independent application
Use a real piece of writing. Ask AI to perform only one stage of the workflow, then review the result for factual accuracy, purpose, reader fit, and authentic voice.

## Completion check
You are ready to continue when you can name the specific writing job assigned to AI and the parts that remain your responsibility.
