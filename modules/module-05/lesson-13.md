# Lesson 13 — When Should I Show an Example?

## Outcome
You can use examples to clarify a pattern or standard without causing AI to copy irrelevant details.

## Direct answer
Provide an example when describing the desired result is less clear than showing a representative one.

Examples are especially useful for:
- style;
- structure;
- classification;
- naming conventions;
- level of detail;
- repeated transformations.

## Tell AI what the example demonstrates
An example works best when you identify the feature to follow.

Instead of:

“Make it like this.”

Use:

“Use this example only as a guide to length and section structure. Do not copy its subject matter or wording.”

This reduces accidental imitation.

## Positive and negative examples
A positive example shows what to do.

A negative example shows what to avoid.

For classification tasks, paired examples can define a boundary more clearly than a long explanation.

Example:
- “Customer asks how to use a feature” → Product question
- “Customer reports the feature is broken” → Technical issue

## Worked example
### Goal
Create short project-risk statements.

### Useful example
“Example pattern: ‘Supplier approval may take longer than planned, which could delay material release.’ Follow the pattern of cause, consequence, and uncertainty. Do not reuse the supplier topic unless it applies.”

The instruction identifies the transferable structure.

## Best practice
Use the fewest examples needed to reveal the pattern, and state which features should or should not be copied.

## Common failure
Learners provide one narrow example and assume AI will infer the full rule. The response may overfit to the example.

When the boundary matters, explain the principle or provide varied examples.

## Guided practice
What should AI copy from this sample meeting summary?

“Decision: Approve the pilot. Owner: Dana. Due: May 12. Open issue: Security review.”

A clear instruction might say:

“Use the same four fields and concise phrasing. Do not preserve the names, dates, or decision.”

## Independent application
Choose a task where an example would genuinely reduce ambiguity. Provide one example and identify the exact feature it demonstrates.

## Completion check
You are ready to continue when the example teaches a transferable pattern rather than becoming an accidental template for unrelated details.
