# Lesson 7 — What Result Do I Need?

## Outcome
You can state the result you want clearly enough for AI to work toward it.

## Direct answer
Begin by naming the result, not merely the topic.

A topic tells the AI what the conversation concerns. A result tells it what useful work should be produced.

Compare:

- Topic: “Retirement planning”
- Result: “Explain the main questions a couple should answer before comparing retirement dates.”

- Topic: “Team meeting”
- Result: “Create a 30-minute agenda that ends with owners and next steps.”

- Topic: “Home repair”
- Result: “Help me organize the symptoms and questions to discuss with a repair technician.”

## A clear result contains an action
Useful action words include:
- explain;
- draft;
- summarize;
- compare;
- organize;
- identify;
- revise;
- plan;
- extract;
- evaluate.

The action should describe what the learner needs at the end of the interaction.

## Worked example
### Vague request
“Help me with my vacation.”

This could mean choosing a destination, estimating cost, planning travel days, creating a packing list, or solving a booking problem.

### Clearer result
“Compare three destinations for a four-day trip and show which one best fits a quiet, low-walking vacation.”

The clearer version does not guarantee a good answer, but it gives the interaction a defined destination.

## Best practice
State the desired result in the first one or two sentences.

## Common failure
A learner supplies a large amount of background before saying what help is needed. The AI may respond to the story rather than produce the intended result.

## Guided practice
Turn each topic into a result.

1. Resume
2. Monthly spending
3. Difficult conversation
4. Learning photography

Possible answers:
1. “Revise my resume summary for a project-management role.”
2. “Group these transactions into recurring bills and flexible spending.”
3. “Help me prepare three points I need to communicate calmly.”
4. “Create a four-week beginner practice plan for learning exposure.”

## Independent application
Write one sentence that states the result you want from a real AI task. Begin with an action word and make the finished product or decision support visible.

## Completion check
You are ready to continue when another person could read your sentence and describe what the AI is expected to produce.
