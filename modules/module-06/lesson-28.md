# Lesson 28 — What Changes When AI Can Search the Web?

## Outcome
You can use web-connected AI to retrieve current information while still evaluating search coverage, source quality, and evidence support.

## Direct answer
Web access can make information more current and traceable, but it does not make every answer complete or correct.

A web-connected system may:
- search for relevant pages;
- open selected results;
- summarize source material;
- compare information across pages;
- provide citations or links.

It may still:
- choose weak sources;
- miss important sources;
- misunderstand a page;
- cite a source that does not support the claim;
- combine facts from different dates or jurisdictions;
- overlook information behind logins, paywalls, or inaccessible files.

## Give the search a job
A good web-research request defines:
- the exact question;
- the required date or time period;
- the relevant location or jurisdiction;
- preferred source types;
- what must be cited;
- whether conflicting evidence should be shown.

Example:

“Find the current carry-on size limits for these three airlines for flights departing the United States. Use each airline's official policy page, note the date checked, and flag any difference between domestic and international rules.”

## Distinguish coverage from certainty
Search results show what the system found, not everything that exists.

For important research, ask:
- Which sources were searched?
- Which authoritative sources were unavailable?
- Are there conflicting dates or definitions?
- What remains uncertain?

## Worked example
### Weak request
“What are the latest rules for renting out my home?”

### Better request
“Find the current short-term-rental registration and operating requirements for a single-family home in [city and state]. Prioritize official city and county sources. Separate confirmed requirements from items that require direct confirmation, and cite each rule.”

The improved request prevents rules from unrelated locations from being blended together.

## Best practice
Use web-connected AI for discovery and synthesis, then inspect the sources supporting important claims.

## Common failure
Learners assume multiple citations mean multiple independent confirmations. Several pages may repeat the same original error or press release.

Look for source independence and authority, not citation count alone.

## Guided practice
Improve this request:

“Find the best laptop for me.”

One possible revision:

“Find currently available laptops under $1,000 for office work, video calls, and light photo editing. Use current manufacturer specifications and at least two reputable independent review sources. Compare battery life, display quality, memory, repairability, and warranty. State where evidence is inconsistent or based on reviewer testing rather than manufacturer claims.”

## Independent application
Write a web-connected research request that includes scope, date sensitivity, source preferences, citation requirements, and an instruction to expose gaps or conflicts.

## Completion check
You are ready to continue when web access is treated as a retrieval capability rather than a guarantee of truth.
