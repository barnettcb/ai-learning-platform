# Lesson 32 — How Do I Manage a Long-Running AI Project?

## Outcome
You can divide an extended project into controlled stages with clear records, review points, and continuation decisions.

## Direct answer
Treat the project as a sequence of governed work sessions, not one endlessly growing conversation.

A durable AI-assisted project needs:
- a stable goal;
- a current scope;
- authoritative source material;
- a list of decisions already made;
- a record of open questions;
- named deliverables;
- review gates before major commitments;
- a clear next action.

The conversation is part of the work, but it should not be the only record of the work.

## Use stages
A practical sequence is:
1. define the outcome and boundaries;
2. gather and organize inputs;
3. design the approach;
4. produce a small test or first section;
5. review against agreed criteria;
6. expand only after the approach works;
7. complete an integration review;
8. archive the final decisions and reusable assets.

Stages prevent a weak early assumption from spreading through the entire project.

## Maintain a project record
Keep a concise source of truth containing:
- current objective;
- deliverables and status;
- constraints;
- approved decisions;
- unresolved issues;
- important evidence;
- rejected approaches and reasons;
- next step.

Update this record when the project changes. Do not rely on memory or expect the AI to reconstruct every prior choice reliably.

## Worked example
A learner builds a household budget over several weeks.

The project record distinguishes verified transactions, proposed categories, assumptions, unresolved expenses, and approved rules. Each session starts from that record, completes one defined task, and ends by updating the status. The learner does not ask AI to “remember everything” without maintaining an external summary.

## Best practice
Before starting a new work session, state the current objective and provide only the prior context needed for that objective.

## Common failure
A project stays inside one expanding chat. Old experiments, rejected ideas, and current instructions become mixed together, making it difficult to know which information still governs the work.

## Guided practice
Outline stages and review gates for creating a community event plan with AI over several sessions.

## Independent application
Choose a real project expected to last more than one session. Create its objective, staged plan, source-of-truth record, first review gate, and next action.

## Completion check
You are ready to continue when the project could resume accurately even after the original conversation is unavailable.
