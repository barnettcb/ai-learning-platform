# Lesson 35 — How Do I Keep Human Judgment and Accountability in Control?

## Outcome
You can identify which parts of AI-assisted work may be delegated and which decisions must remain with a responsible person.

## Direct answer
Delegate preparation, organization, explanation, and challenge where appropriate. Do not delegate authority, accountability, consent, or consequential judgment merely because AI can produce an answer.

Human control is especially important when a task affects:
- health or physical safety;
- finances or legal rights;
- employment or education opportunities;
- privacy and reputation;
- vulnerable people;
- irreversible commitments;
- compliance with policy or law.

## Separate roles explicitly
For consequential work, label the roles:
- **AI role:** organize information, draft, calculate, compare, or identify questions;
- **evidence role:** documents, current sources, measurements, or professional findings;
- **reviewer role:** check accuracy, bias, feasibility, and policy alignment;
- **decision owner:** accept responsibility and authorize action.

One person may hold several human roles, but AI should not be described as the accountable owner.

## Use proportional review
Not every task needs the same control. Match review effort to:
- severity if wrong;
- difficulty of detecting an error;
- sensitivity of the information;
- reversibility of the action;
- number of people affected;
- existence of professional or regulatory requirements.

A low-risk brainstorming list may need a quick review. A medical, financial, personnel, or contractual action needs stronger verification and authorized judgment.

## Worked example
AI drafts a performance warning using notes supplied by a manager.

The system may improve structure and clarity. A responsible manager must still verify every factual statement, ensure prior feedback is represented accurately, follow company policy, remove unsupported conclusions, and approve the final action. Drafting assistance does not transfer accountability.

## Best practice
Before using the output, complete this sentence: “The responsible person is ___, and they must verify ___ before ___ happens.”

## Common failure
A group treats AI involvement as a reason no individual has to own the result. Errors pass through because each person assumes the system or another reviewer handled them.

## Guided practice
Assign roles for this task:

“Use AI to compare insurance options for an employee benefits decision.”

A sound division might be:
- AI organizes supplied plan terms;
- official plan documents provide evidence;
- benefits staff verify interpretation;
- the employee decides based on personal needs;
- qualified advisers answer tax or medical questions.

## Independent application
Choose a consequential AI-assisted task. Write the AI role, evidence sources, reviewer, decision owner, required checks, and the point at which action becomes authorized.

## Completion check
You are ready to continue when you can name the accountable human and the review required before an AI-assisted output affects the real world.
