# Glossary

## AI assistant
A system that produces responses or performs supported actions from user instructions and available information.

## Assumption
Something treated as true without being established by the available evidence.

## Constraint
A limit or requirement the response must follow.

## Context
Background information that changes how the task should be understood or completed.

## Current source
A source recent enough for facts that may change over time.

## Hallucination
A plausible-sounding statement produced without reliable factual support.

## Human review
A responsible person's examination of AI-assisted work before it is used or approved.

## Prompt
The instruction, question, source material, or combination of information given to an AI system.

## Source material
Documents, data, images, or other evidence supplied as the basis for a task.

## Verification
Checking an important claim against suitable evidence.

## Workflow
A repeatable sequence for deciding, performing, reviewing, and preserving work.
