# Privacy Before Sharing

Before entering information into an AI system, ask:

1. Does the task require this information?
2. Can names, account numbers, addresses, or identifiers be removed?
3. Am I authorized to share it?
4. Does my employer or organization restrict use of external AI tools?
5. Could a summary or excerpt accomplish the same result?
6. Would disclosure create harm if the information were exposed or misunderstood?

## Best practice
Share the minimum information needed for the task. When in doubt, use a redacted or fictionalized example and keep the original sensitive material outside the conversation.
