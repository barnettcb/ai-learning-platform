# Output Review Checklist

## Task fit
- The output answers the actual request.
- The intended audience and use are clear.
- All required constraints are addressed.

## Evidence
- Important factual claims are supported.
- Changing information has been checked recently.
- Calculations and quoted terms were independently confirmed where necessary.
- Supplied evidence is distinguishable from generated content.

## Reasoning
- Conclusions follow from the available information.
- Assumptions and uncertainty are visible.
- Alternatives and tradeoffs are represented fairly.

## Completeness
- Required sections and perspectives are present.
- Missing information is identified rather than invented.
- Important limitations are disclosed.

## Usability
- The result is understandable to the intended audience.
- The format supports the next action.
- Wording, tone, and length are appropriate.

## Risk and ownership
- Plausible harm from error has been considered.
- Sensitive information is handled appropriately.
- Required professional, policy, or authorized review is complete.
- A responsible person owns the final decision or action.
